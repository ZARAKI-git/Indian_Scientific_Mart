Who we serve

Four kinds of bench.

Schools

Practical kits against the physics, chemistry and biology syllabus, and steady replacement of whatever a class breaks. In a school lab, that is the recurring cost that matters.

About

That distinction matters when you are buying. What we hold is everyday working stock. What comes with it is a quick, plain quote from a team you can reach directly.
